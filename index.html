What VEX is

Not a chatbot. A runtime that executes missions.

VEX runs real missions against real markets — under rules the model cannot override.

Article I
// ENFORCE

Nothing trades past the safety gate.

Every swap and bridge clears one fail-closed check before it executes, the same way whether the agent is restricted or running full autonomy. When a mission ends on its own, it reports why: deadline reached, capital depleted, max loss hit, no viable opportunity, or emergency stop.

Article II
// PROVE

Every decision leaves a record.

Wins, losses, and blocked attempts all land in the same local log, hashed for dedup, not for show. No screenshots, no cherry-picked highlight reel, and your strategy stays private.

Article III
// LOCAL

Your keys never leave your machine.

Keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and never touch the network, in backup, restore, or anywhere else. Runtime, memory, and logs all live on your hardware. The code is open on GitHub.

How it runs

Four ways to run it.

Every session locks two switches: agent or mission, and restricted or full. Permission is set per session and cannot widen mid-run; an unanswered approval expires in an hour and the mission moves on without it.

AgentRestricted

Agent · Restricted

Ask, approve, done. Anything that would move money pauses for your one-tap approval. The default.

AgentFull

Agent · Full

Ask and it acts. Same one-shot task, no pause. You trade the tap for speed.

MissionRestricted

Mission · Restricted

Autonomy on a leash. It plans and researches freely; the moment it wants to sign, it parks and waits for you.

MissionFull

Mission · Full

Runs for the mission's full duration with no approval prompts. It still can't skip the swap and bridge safety gate, and it still reports its own stop reason when it ends.

Answers

The questions that decide trust.

01Can VEX move funds without me?

+

No. The model can only propose. Every state-changing action clears a fail-closed safety gate before it executes, and in restricted mode nothing signs until you approve it. An unanswered approval expires in an hour and the mission moves on without it.

02Where do my private keys live?

+

On your machine, never transmitted to VEX. Keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and used only at the signing boundary, after an action clears the safety gate. No cloud relay, no custodial server, no remote key escrow.

03Is any of this cloud-dependent?

+

No. VEX is a native desktop app. Runtime, memory, audit logs, and custody all live locally. The only outbound calls are the protocol and model endpoints you explicitly enable.
